Who Can Apply?

The UPDF recruits qualified Ugandan citizens in two main categories

General Recruits

Ugandan citizens aged 18-25 years with at minimum a Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE/O-Level). Must be physically fit and have a clean criminal record.

Professionals

Qualified professionals in ICT, Medicine, Engineering, Law, Education, Aviation, and more. Degree, Diploma, or relevant certification required depending on category.

Sports Personnel

Talented athletes and sports persons with proven track records at national or international level. Must demonstrate excellence in their chosen sporting discipline.

Musicians / Band

Skilled musicians, band specialists, and live performance artists who can contribute to UPDF cultural and ceremonial activities.

Application Process

Complete your application in simple steps

1

Check Eligibility

Ensure you meet all requirements: Ugandan citizenship, valid National ID, age bracket, academic qualifications, and physical fitness criteria.

2

Gather Your Documents

Prepare digital copies of your National ID, passport-size photograph, UCE/UACE certificates, and any professional qualifications.

3

Complete Online Form

Fill out the online application form accurately. Provide personal details, education background, physical attributes, and upload required documents.

4

Save Your Application Number

After submitting, you will receive a unique application number. Keep this safe - you will use it to check your application status.

5

Check Shortlist

Shortlists are published on this portal. Check your status regularly using your application number. Shortlisted candidates will be notified with venue and date.

6

Physical Recruitment & Medical

If shortlisted, report to the assigned recruitment center with original documents, local leader recommendation letters, National ID, and passport photos.
